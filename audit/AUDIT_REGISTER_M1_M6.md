# Audit Register M1–M6

Started 2026-11-19 (Suhail with Claude). One milestone at a time, M1 to M6; decisions on what to do come after M6.

**Lens.** The project objective, from the Nov 2026 CMPDI deck (slides 2–3) and the data-requirements document of 8 July 2026: a physics model of the shovel front end that computes the mechanical torque demand and the external load torque at 30 Hz (tau_load = tau_measured − tau_model), so that machine condition can be separated from motion and load in the motor signals (MT-189, WE-1 physics-based modelling). Findings are judged by what would stop a top-level reviewer from accepting a claim towards that objective.

**Severity.**
- **Critical**: blocks a field-relevant or publishable claim until fixed or stated as a limitation.
- **Major**: weakens a result or its reproducibility.
- **Minor**: housekeeping or documentation; fix when convenient.

**Status.** OPEN until a fix or an accepted-limitation decision is recorded here with a date.

## M1 — 2-DOF rigid model and Stage-1 verification (audited 2026-11-19)

**What M1 is.** A planar two-joint model of the dipper handle (saddle rotation q3, crowd extension d4) derived by Euler–Lagrange, built a second time in Simscape Multibody, and compared: the analytical torques equal the Simscape torques (revolute RMSE 1.35e-6 N m, prismatic 8.2e-11 N, R^2 = 1.000000), and least squares recovers the five revolute parameters exactly when Simscape's own (sensed) accelerations are used (M1/experiments/2026-07-07_stage1_final/stage1_results.mat, script v5.1).

**What holds.** The equations are correct for the stated assumptions and agree with an independently built multibody model to numerical precision. The work caught and recorded its own defects: a fabricated "Rasuli R^2 = 0.942" (struck 2026-07-07), a misread friction column, a geometry bug (v1–v4), a misnamed acceleration signal, a trim switch left off, and the inertia bias from numerical differentiation (Izz 125,804 instead of 287,900). The Nov 2026 deck (slides 12–13) states correctly that the recovered values are the simulation's own inputs.

**M1 verdict.** Correct and honestly documented as verification of the equations. It is not evidence about any real machine, and three choices made here (saddle-torque actuation, Machine A values, shared assumptions) carry into M2–M4.
